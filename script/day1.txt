📅 Day 1: 공원 (Parks)
Q1. [Description]
Question: You indicated in the survey that you enjoy going to parks. Tell me about a park that you often visit. Where is it located and what does it look like? Give me a detailed description of that place.

Script: Oh, that's a tough one, but let me see... You know, there's this park right in my neighborhood that I absolutely adore.

To be specific, it's just, like, a 10-minute walk from my place, and it has this incredibly well-maintained running track. You know, running is all the rage these days in Korea! Whenever I’m stressed out, I just head there and run my heart out. Personally, I’m a huge fan of going there at night because I can really soak up the peace and quiet.

Anyway, long story short, that's pretty much my go-to spot. How about you, Eva?

Q2. [Routine]
Question: I'd like to know what you usually do when you go to the park. Who do you go there with, and what is your typical routine from the moment you arrive until you leave?

Script: Well, to be honest, it really depends on who I’m with.

When I’m alone, I usually just put on my headphones and lose myself in some music while walking. But if I go with my wife, we end up talking for ages. My routine is quite simple, though. First, I do some light stretching—safety first, right? Then, I walk or run for about an hour.

Before heading home, I always grab a bottle of ice-cold water. Honestly, there’s nothing like it after a good workout!

Q3. [Past Experience]
Question: Tell me about a memorable or unexpected incident that happened at a park. When was it, what happened, and why was it so memorable?

Script: Oh! I have such a hilarious story about this.

A few months ago, I was out for a run, and out of nowhere, it started pouring! I thought it would stop soon, so I ducked into a nearby cafe. Well, the rain just wouldn't quit, so I finally gave up and bought an umbrella.

But here is the kicker—less than five minutes later, the rain completely stopped! I was like, "Are you kidding me?" It was a total waste of money, but I guess it’s a funny memory now.
