📅 Day 15: 사회적 이슈 및 변화 (Social Issues & Changes)
Q1. [Comparison - Technology]
Question: How has technology changed the way people communicate compared to the past? Provide some specific examples of these changes.

Script: Well, looking back, the way we communicate has changed tremendously due to technology. In the past, people used to call each other or even write letters, which took a lot of time.

But now, everything is instant. With smartphones and social media apps like KakaoTalk or Instagram, we can message anyone anywhere in the world in a split second. Also, video calls have become very common, making us feel closer to people even if they are far away.

However, I think there’s a downside, too. People sometimes spend too much time on their phones instead of having real face-to-face conversations. So, while technology made things more convenient, it also changed the depth of our interactions.

Q2. [Social Issue - Housing]
Question: There are many issues regarding housing in your country. Tell me about one of those issues. What is the problem, and what are people's concerns about it?

Script: Actually, one of the biggest social issues in Korea these days is the skyrocketing price of housing.

It has become incredibly difficult for young people or newly married couples to buy their own homes, especially in Seoul. The prices are just way too high compared to average salaries. This causes a lot of stress and even leads some people to delay marriage or having children.

The government is trying to solve this by building more apartments, but it doesn’t seem to be enough yet. It’s a very complex problem that everyone is worried about. Personally, I’m also paying close attention to this issue because it affects my future plans as well.
