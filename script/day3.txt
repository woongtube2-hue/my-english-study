📅 Day 3: 집 (My Home)
Q1. [Description]
Question: I’d like to know about where you live. Tell me about your house or apartment. Where is it located? What does it look like? Give me a detailed description of your home.

Script: Well, let me give you a quick tour of my place! I’m currently living in a typical Korean apartment in Seoul, and it’s very cozy.

The layout is pretty standard—a master bedroom, two extra rooms, and a cozy living room. In the living room, I have my favorite piece of furniture: a recliner. It’s honestly the best place to kick back after a long day at the office.

My absolute favorite spot, though, is my computer room. It’s my own private sanctuary where I can focus on my projects. So, overall, I’m very satisfied with my home because it’s just so comfortable.

Q2. [Routine]
Question: What do you usually do at home? Tell me about your typical routine from the moment you wake up or get home. Which room do you spend the most time in?

Script: To tell you the truth, since I work as a programmer, my home is where I truly recharge.

When I get home, I first have dinner with my wife, and then we move to the living room to catch some TV. After that, we usually head to our own rooms for some "me time."

That’s when I go to my computer room to study new technologies. Even on weekends, I try to stick to this self-development routine. So, I’d say I spend most of my time balancing relaxation and personal growth at home.

Q3. [Comparison]
Question: How is your current home different from the house you lived in when you were a child? What are the biggest differences?

Script: Wow, looking back, my home environment has changed drastically.

I grew up in a big house in the countryside with a yard and a rooftop. It was always noisy because I shared a room with my brothers, but it was fun!

Now, I live in a city apartment with no yard. But you know what? The best part is finally having my own private room. I miss the yard sometimes, but I definitely prefer the privacy and comfort of my current home.
