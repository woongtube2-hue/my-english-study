📅 Day 6: 가전제품 (Appliances)
Q1. [Description]
Question: What kind of appliances do you have in your home? Which one do you use most often, and why is it important to you?

Script: Well, honestly, there are two appliances in my house that I absolutely cannot live without.

One is my robot vacuum, and the other is my all-in-one washer and dryer. As a busy person, the robot vacuum is a total life-changer because it cleans the floor automatically while I work.

And the washer-dryer combo is just so convenient. I just press one button and the whole process is done. In short, it's the most useful appliance I have, and I use it every single day.

Q2. [Comparison]
Question: How are household appliances different now compared to the past? What are the biggest changes you have noticed?

Script: It’s actually quite mind-blowing to think about how much appliances have changed over the years.

Back in the day, vacuums were such a hassle—they were heavy and you always had to deal with cords and outlets.

But now, things are so much smarter. I mean, I can control everything from my phone using an app called "SmartThings." Even when I’m not at home, I can start the laundry or the vacuum with just a few taps. Looking back, I think technology has made our lives much more convenient than ever before.

Q3. [Problem/Experience]
Question: Tell me about a time when an appliance broke down or stopped working. What was the problem and how did you fix it?

Script: Oh, I remember this one time during a freezing cold winter when my washing machine stopped working.

The temperature dropped so low that the drain pipe on my balcony completely froze up. Right in the middle of a cycle, the machine just stopped and showed an error message.

I was in a total panic because it was full of wet clothes and freezing water! I had to manually drain the water and use hot water to melt the ice. It was a nightmare at the time, but I’m glad I could fix it myself. Now, I always make sure to keep the balcony warm!
