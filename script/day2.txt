📅 Day 2: 음악 감상 (Music)
Q1. [Description]
Question: You mentioned that you like listening to music. What kind of music do you like? Who is your favorite singer or composer? Tell me about your taste in music in detail.

Script: Well, to be perfectly honest, I'm a big fan of music that really boosts my mood. Whenever I listen to upbeat songs, I feel so much more energetic.

As you probably know, Bruno Mars is my absolute favorite artist. His songs are so catchy that I just can't stop moving! I'm also totally obsessed with his recent song with Lady Gaga, "Die With A Smile." His voice is just so captivating.

So, yeah, those are the vibes I’m really into these days.

Q2. [Routine]
Question: When and where do you usually listen to music? What do you usually do while you are listening to music?

Script: Well, like you said, music is something we can enjoy literally anytime and anywhere.

For me, I especially listen to music during my commute to work. It's the best way to get pumped up before starting my day. Also, since I work as a programmer, I sometimes listen to music while coding to stay in the zone.

But, I make sure not to listen to anything with lyrics when I’m studying. I mean, I keep getting distracted by the words! Other than that, music is basically the soundtrack of my life.

Q3. [Past Experience]
Question: Do you remember a special or memorable time when you listened to a specific song? What was the song, where were you, and why was it so special?

Script: Let me think... I don't have a super dramatic story, but there is one song that always brings back great memories. It’s "Just the Way You Are" by Bruno Mars.

Last year, I went on my honeymoon to Switzerland. While we were traveling, we listened to that song on repeat. The weather was perfect, and that song was just the perfect background music for our trip.

Now, whenever it pops up on my playlist, I feel like I’m right back in Switzerland. It’s definitely my favorite music-related memory.
