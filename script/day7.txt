📅 Day 7: 여행 (Travel)
Q1. [Description]
Question: Where do you usually go for trips in your country? Describe your favorite travel destination and tell me why you like it.

Script: Well, let me see... these days, I actually prefer going to the mountains rather than the beach.

I used to like the ocean more, but my preference has definitely changed over time. One of my favorite memories is going camping with my family to see the stars from the mountainside.

At first, it was so cloudy that I was really disappointed. But then, a miracle happened and the sky cleared up for exactly 30 minutes. In those 30 minutes, I saw more stars than I've seen in my entire life. It was a truly breathtaking and unforgettable experience.

Q2. [Past Experience]
Question: Tell me about the most memorable trip you have ever taken. Where did you go, who were you with, and what happened?

Script: If I had to pick the most memorable trip of my life, it would hands down be my honeymoon to Switzerland.

My wife and I went there last March, and honestly, it felt like a dream. Everything about Switzerland was perfect—from the snowy mountains to the crystal-clear lakes.

We spent most of our time just soaking in the amazing scenery and taking tons of photos. It was the perfect getaway to recharge our batteries. It was the most romantic and relaxing trip I’ve ever had. I still look at those photos whenever I feel stressed at work.

Q3. [Comparison]
Question: How are the trips you take now different from the ones you took when you were a child? How has your travel style changed?

Script: It’s really interesting to see how my travel style has evolved over the years.

When I was a kid, I was a very passive traveler—I just followed my parents on package tours where everything was decided for me.

But now, I strongly prefer "free travel" where I take the lead in planning. I love creating my own schedule and finding hidden spots that are not in the guidebooks. I think I enjoy the process of optimizing my plans because I like everything to be perfect. It’s much more rewarding to travel at my own pace with my wife.
