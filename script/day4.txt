Q1. [Description]
Question: You indicated that you like watching movies. What kind of movies do you like? Who are your favorite actors? Tell me about your taste in movies in detail.

Script: Actually, I’m a huge fan of lighthearted comedy movies.

So, yeah, simple and funny comedy movies are definitely my absolute favorite.

Q2. [Routine]
Question: What do you usually do before and after watching a movie? Do you go to the theater or watch at home? Describe your movie-watching routine.

We have this simple but perfect routine. Before the movie starts, we always order some delivery food—usually fried chicken and beer, which is the best combo! Then, we turn off all the lights to get that theater vibe.

After it's over, we stay up a bit talking about our favorite scenes. It's nothing special, but it's our way of relaxing and spending quality time together.
