📅 Day 5: 조깅 및 걷기 (Jogging)
Q1. [Description]
Question: You mentioned that you go jogging or walking. Where do you usually go for jogging? Describe that place in detail.

Script: Actually, I usually head to a local park near my apartment for a walk or jog.

It’s not a massive park, but it’s always spotless and well-maintained. What I really love is the long walking trail surrounded by trees and flowers—the air is so fresh!

On weekends, it’s full of families and couples, and the atmosphere is just so peaceful. All in all, this park is my favorite spot to clear my head after work.

Q2. [Routine]
Question: When do you usually go for a walk? What do you bring with you? Tell me about your walking routine.

Script: As I mentioned before, I try to go for a walk every single evening after work.

Since I’m a programmer and sit at a desk all day, I really need to get my body moving. I usually go with my wife, and we catch up on each other's day. I always make sure to wear my running shoes and bring my phone.

Lately, I’ve been listening to English podcasts while walking—trying to kill two birds with one stone, you know? In short, this evening walk has become a very important part of my daily life.

Q3. [Past Experience]
Question: Tell me about a memorable experience you had while jogging or walking. What happened and why was it so memorable?

Script: I'll never forget this one time last summer. It’s a bit of a funny story, actually.

The weather was perfectly fine when my wife and I left the house, but out of nowhere, it started raining cats and dogs! We didn't have an umbrella, so we were totally panicking.

We ended up sprinting all the way back home and got completely soaked. We were a mess, but it was such a hilarious and happy ending. It's a great memory we often talk about.
