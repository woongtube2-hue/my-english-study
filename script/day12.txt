📅 Day 12: 국내 여행 (Domestic Travel)
Q1. [Description]
Question: You mentioned in the survey that you enjoy traveling within your own country. Tell me about the places you like to visit. What do they look like, and why do you like going there?

Script: Well, to be honest, I really enjoy traveling to Gangwon Province. It is famous for its beautiful mountains and the East Sea. Whenever I feel suffocated by the city life in Seoul, I head there to get some fresh air.

The scenery there is just breathtaking. In the morning, I can see the sunrise over the ocean, and in the afternoon, I can go for a hike in the mountains. It’s the perfect place to enjoy nature. Plus, the seafood there is incredibly fresh and delicious!

I think it's the best place to visit because it offers both relaxation and great food. That’s why I try to go there at least once a year.

Q2. [Routine]
Question: Tell me about what you usually do when you go on a domestic trip. From the moment you start planning until you come back home, what is your typical process?

Script: That's an interesting question. My travel routine is actually quite organized.

First, I start by searching for a nice place to stay, like a cozy pension or a hotel with a good view. Once the accommodation is booked, I look up famous local restaurants on Instagram or YouTube. I’m a big foodie, so finding good places to eat is my top priority!

During the trip, I usually spend my time exploring the local area and taking lots of photos. I’m not the type of person who likes a tight schedule; I prefer to just go with the flow.

On the last day, I always buy some local specialties to bring back home. It’s a great way to wrap up the trip and share the experience with others.

Q3. [Past Experience]
Question: Tell me about a memorable experience you had during a trip within your country. What happened, and why was it so special or unexpected? Give me all the details.

Script: I remember a trip I took to Jeju Island a few years ago. I went there during the winter, expecting it to be relatively warm, but a massive snowstorm hit the island suddenly.

The airport was actually shut down for a whole day, and I was stranded at my hotel. At first, I was quite frustrated because I had so many things planned. However, I decided to just enjoy the situation. I spent the day drinking hot cocoa in the hotel lounge and watching the snow fall over the palm trees. It was a very unique and beautiful sight that you don't see often in Korea.

Even though my plans were ruined, it became one of my most memorable trips because the scenery was so magical. It taught me that sometimes, the best travel memories are the ones you didn't plan for.
