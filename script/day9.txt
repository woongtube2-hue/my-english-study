📅 Day 9: 롤플레잉 심화 (Role-play)
Q1. [Problem Solving]
Question: I'm sorry, but there is a problem you need to resolve. You were supposed to go to a performance with your friend today, but something has come up and you cannot make it. Call your friend, explain the situation, and offer two or three alternatives to solve the problem.

Script:
Hi there, it's me. Listen, I have some bad news. I was really looking forward to the show today, but something urgent came up at the last minute, so I don't think I can make it. I’m so sorry about this!

Since I already have the tickets, I was thinking of a few ways to fix this. First, do you think we can change the ticket date to next weekend? I checked the website, and there are still some seats left for next Saturday.

If that doesn't work for you, how about I give my ticket to another friend? That way, you don't have to go alone. I feel terrible for canceling like this, so next time, dinner is on me! Let me know what you think.

Q2. [Past Experience]
Question: Have you ever had a similar experience where you had a problem with a performance or an event you had planned to attend? What exactly happened, and how did you deal with the situation? Tell me everything in as much detail as possible.

Script:
Actually, I remember a time when I had a similar issue a couple of years ago. I had booked tickets for a popular musical with my best friend.

However, on the day of the show, there was a huge snowstorm. The roads were completely frozen, and the public transportation was a mess. There was no way we could make it to the theater on time. I was so disappointed because we had been waiting for that show for months.

To handle the situation, I immediately called the ticket office. Luckily, they told me that many people were having the same problem due to the weather. They were kind enough to offer a full refund or a date change. I chose to change the date to the following week, and we eventually got to see the show.

Looking back, it was a very stressful day, but I’m glad it all worked out in the end.
