📅 Day 8: 롤플레잉 기초 (Role-play)
Q1. [Situation]
Question: You are planning a trip for next week. Call a travel agency and ask three or four questions about a travel package.

Script: Hi there! I'm calling to ask a few questions about your travel packages.

I'm planning a trip for next week, so could you tell me the exact itinerary for the 3-day package? Also, I was wondering about the price. What is the total price per person, and are there any extra fees I should know about?

Oh, one more thing. Can I book for three people next Friday? And just in case, could you tell me where your office is located? I might want to visit in person.

Anyway, that sounds great. Thanks for the information! I'll talk to my friends and call you back.

Q2. [Problem Solving]
Question: You called a restaurant to make a reservation, but they are fully booked. Suggest two or three alternatives to solve the problem.

Script: Oh, I see. It's a shame that you're fully booked at that time. I was really looking forward to eating there because I heard so many great things about your food.

Then, do you have any other time today? Or maybe tomorrow? My schedule is quite flexible, so any other time would be fine for me. I just really want to try your signature dish.

If not, is there a waiting list I can join? Could you give me a call if there’s a last-minute cancellation? We can wait nearby, so please let me know.

Anyway, thank you for checking the schedule for me. I'll talk to my friend about these options and see what we can do. I'll call you back soon!
